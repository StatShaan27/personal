Projects & reading.

Supervised research, reading projects, and coursework through which I’m learning statistics and its applications. Reports, code, and presentations are linked where available.

01

Prof. Aritra Bhattacharya | 2026 – Present

Single-Cell RNA Sequencing Analysis of Fibrotic Lung Immune Cells

An ongoing undergraduate thesis under Prof. Aritra Bhattacharya’s supervision. I’m learning to work with single-cell RNA sequencing data from fibrotic lung tissue, using R, Seurat, and Bioconductor to explore immune-cell populations and gene-expression patterns.

Sun Yat-Sen University Summer School Group Project | 2026

Model Discordance as a Clinical Complexity Marker in Type 2 Diabetes Risk Prediction

A summer-school group project exploring disagreement between models for Type 2 Diabetes risk prediction. This was an opportunity to practise health-data analysis and think about how different modeling choices affect predictions.

A Statistical Methods IV group project in which we studied Kernelized Spatial Depth (KSD) and compared depth-based approaches to outlier detection. I used the project to learn about robust methods, kernel-based ideas, and their empirical evaluation.

An eight-week supervised study of Galton–Watson branching processes and their long-term behaviour at IISER Pune under Dr. Moumanti Podder. The project helped me work through classical probability theory and write a structured report.

A microbiology coursework project with Prof. Aritra Bhattacharya, examining SARS-CoV-2 genomic data from India during 2020–2021. I practised working with GISAID data, tracking mutation patterns including D614G and Delta, and presenting the analysis in R.

Bayesian Decision Theory vs Classical and Machine Learning Paradigms

A supervised reading project with Prof. Sourabh Bhattacharya. I’m learning Bayesian decision theory and comparing its ideas with frequentist and machine-learning approaches, including questions about risk, likelihood, and hypothesis testing.
