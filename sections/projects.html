Research & projects.

Biological applications grounded in statistical methodology. Reports, code, and presentations are linked where available.

01

Prof. Aritra Bhattacharya | 2026 – Present

Single-Cell RNA Sequencing Analysis of Fibrotic Lung Immune Cells

In-depth analysis of single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) data to study immune cell populations under fibrotic lung conditions. Applied high-dimensional statistical methods to identify immune subpopulations and characterize transcriptional programs. Developed computational workflows in R (Seurat, Bioconductor) integrating statistical inference with biological interpretation.

Depth-Based Outlier Detection in High-Dimensional Data

Study of Kernelized Spatial Depth (KSD) for detecting outliers in high-dimensional datasets. Combined robust statistical depth functions with kernel-based methods to capture nonlinear structures. Implemented and empirically evaluated depth-based algorithms on complex data.

Bayesian Decision Theory vs Classical and Machine Learning Paradigms
